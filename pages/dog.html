The Doggone Minimal Website (DMW) is a cleaned-up template. Credit: crrdlx. It traces back to the Perfect ‘Foul-Mouthed’ Website (strong language).

Source: github.com/crrdlx/doggone-minimal-website. A static reference copy lives at dmw.html in this repo.

It’s just added code. And it’s really clean and simple.

This multi-page app reuses the same typography and table rules as that single-page template.

A little more accessibility

You should take care of people. The HTML root should include a lang attribute on your pages (this site uses lang="en").
Have you ever listened to a wrong language screen reader lecture? What a bad experience.

Typography matters

Don’t be dumb with typography. Don’t let improper marks and quotes make their way onto websites because of dumb defaults in applications and CMSs.
And did you let the browser choose the font? Don’t make someone read long body text in Times New Roman unless you mean to.

This paragraph requests a different font stack (browser-dependent).
